# Krownly Support

Krownly is an iPhone app for privately recording and reviewing hair and crown progress. Account information, videos, and analysis history stay on your device unless you choose to save a copy of a video to Photos.

## Contact

For help with Krownly, email [eddiejjmorrissey@gmail.com](mailto:eddiejjmorrissey@gmail.com).

## Common Questions

### How do I create an account?

Tap **Sign Up** on the first screen and enter an email address and password. Krownly accounts are local to your iPhone and are not sent to a server.

### Why can't I record a video?

Open **Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → Krownly** and enable Camera access. Enable Microphone access if you want audio in recordings.

### How do I save a video to Photos?

Open Krownly Settings and enable **Auto-save to Photos**. iOS will ask for permission the first time Krownly saves media.

### How do I delete my account and data?

Open **Krownly → Settings → Delete Account**. This removes your local account, saved videos, and analysis history from Krownly. Copies previously saved to Photos remain in Photos and can be deleted there.
